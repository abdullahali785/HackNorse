ᚨ// 01 · about

Luther's first hackathon

HackNorse is Luther College's first-ever hackathon: a 24-ish-hour sprint where you team up, pick a track, and build something real from scratch.

It's completely free, no coding experience is required, and it's open to every major and every skill level, from first-time coders to seasoned devs. Bring your curiosity, and leave with a working project, new friends, and maybe a prize.

  • ~24hours to build
  • 2–3people per team
  • 4tracks to choose from
  • $0to enter

ᛏ// 05 · rules & judging

The law of the longhouse

Rules

  1. Teams of 2–3. No solo entries, no teams of four.
  2. Luther students only. Every team member must be a current Luther College student.
  3. Fresh code only. All code must be written during the event. Open-source libraries and APIs are fine.
  4. Pick one track. Choose Marketing, Sustainability, Healthcare, or Fintech. You can switch any time before you submit.
  5. Submit by 3:00 PM on Nov 21. Email a link to your GitHub repo and a demo video to css@luther.edu.

ᛇ// 06 · faq

Questions, answered

Do I need coding experience?

Nope. HackNorse is open to every major and every skill level. Designers, writers, and business and science majors all bring a lot to a team, and you'll learn as you go. First hackathon? You're in good company. This is Luther's first one too.

What if I don't have a team?

Sign up solo! Mention it on the sign-up form and we'll try to match you with teammates before the event. There's also a team-matching session right after the opening ceremony.

How are teams formed?

Teams are 2–3 current Luther students. You can form your own team with friends, get matched through the sign-up form, or find teammates at the team-matching session on Friday.

What should I bring?

Your laptop and charger, plus headphones, a water bottle, and anything else that helps you work. Bring your phone if you're building a mobile app. Olin 102 closes overnight, so make sure your setup is easy to carry back to your dorm.

Is food provided?

We'll have snacks and drinks to keep you going. Meals aren't provided, so plan to eat on your own schedule.

Can I switch tracks?

Yes. You can change tracks any time up until the submission deadline. Just name your final track when you submit.

Do I have to stay overnight?

No. Olin 102 closes overnight, so everyone heads back to their dorm. Keep hacking from there if you want, but get some sleep too. Rested brains build better demos.

Who can I contact?

Email us at css@luther.edu or DM @css_club_luther on Instagram (opens in a new tab) on Instagram.
